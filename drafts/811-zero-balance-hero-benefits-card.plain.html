A Kotak811 zero balance digital savings account gives you the freedom to bank entirely on your terms. Whether you are opening a digital savings account for the first time or switching to a more flexible option, Kotak811 makes zero balance account opening online quick and completely paperless. All you need is your Aadhaar and PAN card to get started. Once your digital bank account is active, you get access to a free virtual debit card, free fund transfers via NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, and UPI, and the ability to earn up to 5%* interest p.a. through ActivMoney - all without maintaining any minimum balance. From everyday payments to long-term savings, your Kotak811 zero balance savings account is built to do it all.
